About NoteCast

Empowering students, engineers, and researchers worldwide with autonomous AI study notes.

NoteCast was built out of a shared frustration with traditional online learning: the exhausting loop of pausing educational videos every few seconds, frantically transcribing whiteboard notes, and missing the overarching lecture flow.

Our Mission

We leverage advanced AI vision and transcription models to eliminate cognitive overload. By capturing key slides, diagrams, and audio in real-time, NoteCast lets you focus 100% on understanding concepts while automatically synthesizing comprehensive master study guides, PDFs, and presentation decks.

Built for the Modern Learner

Whether you're mastering advanced computer science architecture, preparing for medical exams, or reviewing complex lectures, NoteCast is designed to scale with your academic workload.
