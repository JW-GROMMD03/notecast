Fins increase heat transfer area to a low-h fluid (like air). Efficiency drops as the fin gets longer, thinner, or lower-k. Effectiveness must exceed 1 for the fin to be worthwhile — if ε < 1, just use the base area. Adding a fin always increases heat transfer, but with diminishing returns.
