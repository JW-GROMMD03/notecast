Injection molding: melt plastic is injected into a closed mold, packed under pressure, cooled, and ejected. Cycle time dominated by cooling. Wall thickness drives cooling time (t²). Thin walls + long flow paths = difficult to fill (short shots). Thick sections = sink marks.
