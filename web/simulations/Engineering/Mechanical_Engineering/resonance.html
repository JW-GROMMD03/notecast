Resonance occurs when drive frequency ω matches natural frequency ω_n. Amplitude magnifies by Q = 1/(2ζ). Lightly damped systems have huge magnification — the Tacoma Narrows Bridge collapsed at ζ ≈ 0.01 with M ≈ 50. Damping is your defense against resonance.
