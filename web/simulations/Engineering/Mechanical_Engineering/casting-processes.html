M = V/A (casting modulus)
Riser must solidify LAST
During casting, liquid metal is poured into a mold and must solidify. Shrinkage during cooling causes porosity if no riser feeds liquid metal. Chvorinov says thick sections solidify slowly. The riser must have higher modulus than the casting to solidify last and feed shrinkage.
