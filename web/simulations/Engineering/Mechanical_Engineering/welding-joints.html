Allowable shear ≈ 0.3·Sut
Welding joins metals by melting (or fusing) them. Fillet welds are sized by their throat (0.707 × leg). Butt welds carry tension across the full section. Heat input controls HAZ size and residual stress. Too hot: distortion and cracking. Too cold: cold lap, incomplete fusion.
