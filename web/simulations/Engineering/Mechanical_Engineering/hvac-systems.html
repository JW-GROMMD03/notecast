Psychrometrics describes moist air properties. Dry-bulb, wet-bulb, dew point, relative humidity, humidity ratio, and enthalpy are linked. HVAC coils cool air, often below dew point — moisture condenses out. SHR (sensible heat ratio) tells how much cooling goes to temperature vs. dehumidification.
