τ = I·α (Newton's 2nd for rotation)
L = I·ω (angular momentum)
KE = ½·I·ω² (rotational KE)
Moment of inertia I depends on mass distribution. Moving mass away from the axis increases I quadratically with r. A ring has twice the I of a disk of same mass and radius. This is why a hollow tube resists rotation more than a solid rod.
