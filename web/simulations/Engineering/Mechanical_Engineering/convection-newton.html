λ_max = 2898 / T (μm·K)
Every surface radiates. The rate depends on T⁴ — extremely sensitive to temperature. A polished (low-ε) surface is a poor emitter/absorber. This is why spacecraft use polished gold foil. Wien's law tells us hotter objects peak at shorter wavelengths (red glow → white hot).
