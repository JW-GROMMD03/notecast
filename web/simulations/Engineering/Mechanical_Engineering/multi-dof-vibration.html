ω₁, ω₂: natural frequencies
Mode shape: eigenvector {φ}
A 2-DOF system has TWO natural frequencies and TWO mode shapes. Mode 1: masses move in phase (lower frequency). Mode 2: masses move out of phase (higher frequency). Any free vibration is a combination of both modes. This generalizes to N-DOF.
