Q̇ = k·A·ΔT/L (Fourier)
R_th = L/(k·A) (thermal resistance)
Q̇ = ΔT / ΣR_th (series network)
Heat conducts from hot to cold, proportional to temperature gradient, area, and thermal conductivity k. Low-k materials (insulation, wood) are good insulators. High-k materials (metals) are good conductors. Multiple layers in series add their resistances.
