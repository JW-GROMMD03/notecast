Springs store energy elastically. Spring rate k is set by wire diameter d, coil diameter D, and active coils Na. Doubling wire diameter multiplies rate by 16! Wahl factor K_w accounts for the stress concentration from coil curvature. Springs must be designed to avoid solid height, buckling, and surge.
