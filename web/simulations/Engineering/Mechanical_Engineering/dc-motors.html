ω_stall = 0, T_stall = V·Kt/Ra

A DC motor converts electrical to mechanical power. Back-EMF opposes supply voltage as speed builds. Stall current is huge (V/Ra) — this is why motors need current limiting. Speed control via PWM: average voltage = duty × V_supply. Torque is proportional to current.
