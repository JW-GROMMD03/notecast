P + ½ρv² + ρgz = const
A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (continuity)
Bernoulli's equation states that along a streamline, pressure + kinetic + potential energy is conserved. Faster flow means lower pressure. This explains lift, carburetors, and why shower curtains blow inward. Valid for incompressible, inviscid, steady flow.
