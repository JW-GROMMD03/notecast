FDM: anisotropic (weaker Z)
AM builds parts layer by layer from digital models. FDM extrudes thermoplastic filament. SLA cures liquid resin with UV laser. SLS/SLM fuse powder. Each has trade-offs in speed, resolution, material properties, and cost. Layer adhesion makes parts anisotropic — weaker perpendicular to layers.
