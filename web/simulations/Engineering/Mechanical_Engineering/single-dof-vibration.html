ω_n = √(k/m) (natural frequency)
ζ = c/(2√(km)) (damping ratio)
r = ω/ω_n (frequency ratio)
Single-DOF vibration: mass-spring-damper. ζ < 1 underdamped (oscillates), ζ = 1 critically damped, ζ > 1 overdamped. When r = 1 (ω = ω_n), resonance — amplitude grows to Q = 1/(2ζ) times static deflection. This is how bridges collapse.
