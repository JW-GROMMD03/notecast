Re = ρvD/μ = vD/ν
Re < 2300: laminar flow
Re > 4000: turbulent flow
Reynolds number is the ratio of inertial to viscous forces. Low Re: smooth layered flow (laminar). High Re: chaotic mixing (turbulent). Transitional zone between 2300–4000 is unpredictable. This single number predicts flow behavior across all scales.
