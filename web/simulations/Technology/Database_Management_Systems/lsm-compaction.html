💡 WHY LSM-TREES WIN narrative
1. Writes are ALWAYS sequential (WAL + append).
2. Memtable absorbs bursts at RAM speed.
3. Flush writes sorted SSTables — sequential I/O.
4. Background compaction merges files.
5. Reads hit memtable first, then SSTables top-down.
6. Trade-off: read amplification + write amplification.
Used by: RocksDB, LevelDB, Cassandra, ScyllaDB, HBase, BadgerDB, TiKV.
