🔁 The Three Phases
1. ANALYZE Scan WAL forward from last checkpoint. Rebuild the transaction table and the dirty page table. Identify winners (committed) and losers (uncommitted).
2. REDO Replay ALL updates (including from losers), from the smallest recLSN forward. Skip pages where pageLSN ≥ LSN. Uses "repeating history" — the DB state becomes exactly what it was at crash.
3. UNDO Roll back losers in reverse LSN order. Write CLRs (Compensation Log Records) so undo is itself idempotent — a crash during recovery can restart without redoing completed undos.
